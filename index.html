About these receipts

Three unrelated files were used: a Spotify play history (2013 to 2024), a household ledger in INR (2015 to 2018), and a synthetic bank-card file (2022 to 2024). They don't belong to one person. Treating them as one is a storytelling choice.

Spotify times are UTC, so the clock chart has a slider instead of a claim.
